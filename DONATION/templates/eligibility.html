Eligibility Criteria

  • Age: 18 – 65 years
  • Weight: minimum 50 kg
  • Hemoglobin: ≥ 12.5 g/dL
  • BP: 90–160 / 60–100 mmHg
  • Last donation: at least 90 days ago
  • No recent illness, surgery, or medication
